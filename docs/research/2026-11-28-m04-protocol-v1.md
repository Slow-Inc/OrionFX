## 1. What the research changed (summary; full notes and sources: `targets_research.md`, attached to #132)

- **Accuracy and break-even.** Credible out-of-sample directional accuracy for intraday FX, gold and equities ML is about **50–55%**. Break-even is p_side − p_opposite > cost/ATR. With stressed costs and ATR(M15) of $3–6, the break-even hit rate is about 54–59% (FLAT share up to 30%), and higher under stressed cost. A fixed "≥ 53% accuracy" target is therefore not an economic gate.
- **Three-class edge.** `(2p−1)` overstates the edge when FLAT has probability mass. EV/ATR = p_side − p_opposite − cost/ATR (implemented in #129).
- **Labels are a proxy for real exits.** The system has **no per-position SL/TP** (master plan RSK-05). Its real exits are the Emergency SL, the USD cycle target and basket trailing. The ±1·ATR triple-barrier label is a proxy. Only the **system tier** (#131), which replays the real exits, can show that a model is usable.
- **Sample size.**
  - 200 trades lets luck through. With no skill, the chance that one of 20 configs reaches PF ≥ 1.15 is 16–61%.
  - A deflated Sharpe ≥ 0.95 across 20 configs at a per-trade Sharpe of about 0.05–0.07 needs thousands of out-of-sample trades.
  - The calculations were re-run in this session: `calc.py`, reproduced.
- **Live haircut.** Expect live Sharpe at about half of backtest. A backtest Sharpe barely predicts live Sharpe (Wiecki et al.: R² < 0.025).

**Expected outcome, stated now.** The research puts realistic accuracy at or below break-even, so the **likeliest v1 result is "did not meet targets"**. Under the goal, an honest failure is a complete result, just as a pass would be.

## 2. Data windows (pre-registered)

| Window | Range | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Debug window | first 12 months of available history (e.g. 2016-01 to 2016-12) | Free to read for debugging pipelines and features. It is **never** used for claims, and nothing tuned here counts as evidence. |
| Walk-forward | after the debug window, up to the holdout start | Expanding train window, **6-month test folds** (about 15 folds), embargo ≥ the longest label horizon, purge by label end. All configs are judged here. |
| Holdout (single use) | the **last 18 months** of available history (e.g. 2025-06 to 2026-11) | It is opened **once**, by a recorded step, after the power check. Sized on power grounds: 12 months is underpowered. It is **not** moved to avoid the October 2025 and January 2026 crashes, because those are the coverage the owner asked for. After it is opened it becomes development data. |

The exact dates are fixed in the hashed protocol record at import time, from the actual coverage of the permitted data source (#128). The rule above is what is certified.

## 3. Binding gates (assumption-light; all must pass)

All "lower bound" (LB) values are one-sided 95% **block bootstrap over trading days** (block length 5 days, 10,000 resamples). They are computed on the pooled walk-forward test predictions or trades, at **stressed cost**: spread at the era's 75th percentile + commission + slippage × 1.5.

| # | Tier | Gate | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Model | Multiclass Brier skill against training-fold priors: pooled LB > 0, **and** ≥ 12 of 15 folds positive (sign test p ≈ 0.018), **and** no fold below −0.01 | "Every fold > 0" is too strict for noisy folds. The sign test plus the pooled LB is robust. |
| G2 | Model | Calibration: ECE ≤ 0.02 (15 equal-mass bins) on pooled test predictions, **and** on traded predictions the realised edge's LB is above cost/ATR within the traded probability range | EV depends on calibrated p. The check is done where trades happen. |
| G3 | System (#131, real exits) | Net expectancy per trade: LB > 0 at stressed cost | The economic gate. |
| G4 | System | Deflated Sharpe ratio ≥ 0.95 with N = **configs actually tried** (counter in the run log) | Corrects for selection across the budget. |
| G5 | System | PBO ≤ 0.2 (CSCV over the config × fold matrix) | Probability of backtest overfitting. |
| G6 | Coverage | No adequately sized regime bucket (§4) is significantly negative: one-sided test of expectancy < 0, Holm-corrected across buckets, p < 0.05 | Coverage of market conditions. |

**Reachability check before the first real run.** Measure the baseline's trade rate on the debug window, then compute whether G3/G4 are reachable at that rate over the walk-forward span. If they are not reachable, record that up front: it predicts "did not meet targets".

**Diagnostics** (reported, **not** gating, because the evidence for their levels is thin):
- direction accuracy on non-FLAT outcomes
- precision and coverage by probability band
- profit factor (reference 1.15 at stressed cost in walk-forward, 1.10 on the holdout)
- break-even cost/ATR per era
- bootstrapped 95th-percentile drawdown. M06 DD numbers are still TBD; the reference is ≤ 50% of the user's max-DD.

## 4. Regime rule (fixed now; buckets need ≥ 300 trades and ≥ 60 trading days)

- **Volatility:** daily realised volatility as a percentile of the trailing 252 trading days. Low < 33, mid 33–66, high > 66.
- **Trend:** z-score of the 20-day return against the trailing 252-day distribution. |z| > 1 gives trend-up or trend-down; otherwise range.
- **Session (New York time):** Asia 18:00–02:00, London 02:00–08:00, New York 08:00–17:00. Rollover 17:00–18:00 is **no-trade** (the session schedule already makes it stale).
- **Era:** 2016–2021, and 2022–end (central-bank buying above 1,000 t a year; record highs).
- **Named stress windows**, reported individually:
  - March 2020
  - 2022 rate hikes
  - April 2025 tariff shock
  - 20–21 October 2025
  - 30 January 2026

  The research verified the 2025 and 2026 dates. The others are marked unverified.
- **News windows** (tier-1 US releases ±30 min) are **not gated in v1**: no point-in-time calendar source exists yet (#41 / OD-14). This is reported as a coverage gap.
- **Adding a no-trade gate** for a weak bucket is a **config**: it spends budget and is pre-declared before it is tested.

## 5. Budget, logging, terminal states

- **Budget:** **20 configs** in total. Feature set, label version, horizon, weighting, calibration method, model family, hyperparameter set and no-trade gate each count as configs. Every run is logged, failures included, with the config counter.
- **Order:** no fit on walk-forward folds before the hashed protocol record and the run log exist (#132 minimal) and the system tier (#131) is built. Before that, only the debug window may be read.
- **Holdout opening:** only after a config passes G1–G6 in walk-forward **and** the power check shows the holdout can confirm it (expected LB > 0 under the walk-forward effect size).
- **Terminal states:**
  - **Pass:** G1–G5 hold on the holdout (LB > 0), and G6 holds where buckets are adequate. The model becomes a candidate, is shadowed beside the champion, and is promoted to Shadow/Demo under ADR 0001.
  - **Inconclusive:** holdout point estimates are positive but an LB is ≤ 0. The planned next step is a Shadow extension, which is **unreachable while #17 is blocked**. Record it and stop.
  - **Fail:** a holdout point estimate is ≤ 0, or G1 fails, or the budget is exhausted in walk-forward. **Stop and report.** Targets are never lowered.
- **v2 rule:** a v2 after a v1 failure may only be judged on data **nobody read under v1**. Without a forward feed (#17), a v1 failure means stop and report. It does not mean retrain on the same history.

## 6. Promotion and kill switches (Shadow/Demo; feed OD-08 for Live)

- **Stages:**
  - Shadow: ≥ 3 months and ≥ 300 signals.
  - Demo: ≥ 3 months and ≥ 300 trades. Demo can only rule out a fake edge; proving an edge after the haircut would need about 5,000 trades.
  - Live: owner only (OD-08).
- **Kill switches** (on top of the M06 lock; never auto-resume):
  - drawdown beyond the bootstrapped 95th percentile, or underwater longer than 3× the expected time;
  - live significantly worse than 0.5× backtest (rolling test);
  - realised edge below cost/ATR;
  - realised costs above 1.5× the modelled costs;
  - feature drift PSI > 0.25.

## 7. Evidence strength and what is thin

- **High:** the break-even math; the multiple-testing and deflated-Sharpe method.
- **Medium:** Brier/ECE practice; the regime method.
- **Low:** PF levels, stage durations, the kill-switch cut-offs. That is why these are diagnostics or owner input, not gates.
- **No rigorous study** reports gold accuracy at M15–H1.
