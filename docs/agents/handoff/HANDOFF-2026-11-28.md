## 2. Decisions made this session

All of these were made by the agent under the owner's delegation and recorded in the docs on the branches.
- **Labels tb.v2.**
  - One exhaustive UP/DOWN/FLAT label per row, symmetric ±TARGET_ATR·ATR_M15 on the bid/trade path, no costs inside the label.
  - Dual touch is AMBIGUOUS and excluded. A gap through a barrier at the open is labelled by that barrier.
  - Why tb.v1 was rejected: it was side-dependent and spread-dependent.
- **EV.** `size·(d·(p_s − p_opp) − (spread + 2·SLIPPAGE)) − commission`. FLAT drift is 0 (a certified assumption). Missing input gives None, which means WAIT.
- **#154 M2 rule, still to implement.** Write `dual_touch_count`, `dual_touch_rate` and `flat_share` into the export manifest, per era (era = each split, plus "all"). If the rate exceeds 1% in any era, the next label version adds AMBIGUOUS as a 4th class, priced worst-case: `EV = size·(d·(p_s − p_o − p_amb) − fills) − comm`.
- **#154 M1, still to implement.** `session` becomes a required keyword in `export_dataset`/`prediction_labels`. Add `session:{schedule_id|24x7}` to source_refs. Add a parity test with XAUUSD_NY_1700 on both sides.
- **#154 M3, still to implement.** Export `ends_at`; folds purge on `ends_at`.
- **Cost.** STRESSED_COST is $1.07/oz in fixed real dollars. On ratio-adjusted GC it is applied as $1.07·f(t). It is not price- or ATR-scaled.
- **Viability and barrier width.** Viability is judged on the most recent development era (2022 to the start of the holdout), using price and label stats only (no model), on development data only.
  - The k grid {1.0, 1.25, 1.5, 2.0} at H1: pick the smallest k that passes V1 (FLAT ≤ 60%) and V2 (median stressed cost/d ≤ 0.10).
  - The chosen k ships as a new label version plus a TARGET_ATR change through risk review, before the hash.
  - If no k qualifies, the result is non-viable and the next step is a v2 proposal.
- **ECE gate.** Class-wise ECE_sweep (L1) on pooled OOS, threshold = q50 of the consistency-resampling noise floor + 0.02, with a per-fold sanity bound. Implemented in #155.
- **Data source.** FirstRate Data GC 1m, ratio-adjusted continuous series, US Eastern bar-start timestamps, the CME Globex schedule, and NO_TRADE fills for gaps of 15 minutes or less. Dukascopy, Polygon, Twelve Data and Databento are rejected (restrictive terms); HistData, OANDA and the Kaggle sets are unclear. Notes: `docs/research/2026-11-28-xauusd-data-sources.md` on #156. GC is a proxy, so **spot parity against broker MT5 data is required before Shadow**.
- **Colab registration.** Option A: no registry write from the runner, only verified files plus `run.json`. VM-claimed LIVE/calibrated/validation are refused. Metrics must be recomputed locally.
